# SkillProof MX — Product Charter · Week 12

Founder: Valeria Karam Galland. Chapter: W4, Twin Sparrows. Venture: SkillProof MX.

## Problem and user
A recent graduate in Mexico needs to demonstrate entry-level financial Excel skills through inspectable work. A reviewer needs to see the calculation, its evidence and what remains uncertain before making a consequential judgment.

## Product promise
Convert one fictional Excel exercise into a cell-linked evidence report. A score describes one task, never the person. Improvement and a new attempt remain available.

## Core and accountability
Upload → validate → inspect calculations and dependencies → classify verified, contradictory or pending → export report. Financial interpretation and hiring remain human decisions. No operational reviewer or deadline is promised until an owner and capacity exist.

## Success
Before the module closes, the declared domain loads, a user signs in with Google, uploads the assessment, receives an evidence report, saves it to their own Supabase rows, reloads it and exports it. Another user cannot read it.

## Conditions
Distinguish facts, assumptions, unknowns and simulations. No 10:1 claim before measured total-effort and quality comparison against manual review and a basic spreadsheet checker. No permanent candidate label, surveillance, automatic hiring or unsupported human-service promises.

## Business unknowns
Payer identity, price, budget authority, employer acceptance and review capacity are not validated. Domain ownership and deployment accounts must be confirmed.

## Three-year direction
SkillProof MX could support a small library of employer-validated role assessments in Mexico. Candidates would control their latest evidence and reviewers would retain accountable judgment. Expansion would depend on measured validity, fairness, demand and operational capacity.
